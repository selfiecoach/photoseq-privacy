PhotoSeq Privacy Policy

PhotoSeq: Arrange & Rename · Android app (package com.photoseq.app)
Effective date: 5 November 2026

In short
  • Your photos, file names and projects never leave your device. We don’t upload, collect or see them.
  • There is no account, no analytics and no crash reporting.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and related data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in the app.

This policy explains what information PhotoSeq (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies only to the PhotoSeq Android app.

1. Your photos and files

PhotoSeq lets you put photos in an exact order and give them sequential file names. To do this it only accesses the photos and folders you choose with Android’s own system pickers (the Photo Picker and the Storage Access Framework). The app does not scan your gallery and does not request broad storage permissions.

  • All arranging, renaming and copying happens on your device. Photos are read only to show thumbnails and to copy their exact bytes to new files.
  • When you save renamed copies, they are written to Pictures/PhotoSeq or to a folder you pick. Your originals are not changed.
  • If you choose “Rename originals”, only the file names in the folder you selected are changed, and only after you confirm. Image contents are never modified.
  • Photos, file names, folder names and image metadata are never uploaded to us or to any third party, including the ad network.

If you pick photos stored by a cloud service (for example a cloud gallery), that service may use the internet to deliver the file to your device under its own privacy policy. PhotoSeq itself does not send the photo anywhere.

2. Information stored on your device

To let you continue later and recover safely from interruptions, the app stores the following in its private storage on your device only:

  • Your projects: project name, the order of photos, their file names, sizes and dates, and links (content URIs) to the photos you selected, plus the access permissions Android granted for them.
  • Your naming pattern and save-location choices.
  • A private log of file operations (old name, new name, result) used for recovery and the in-app activity log.
  • App settings such as theme, thumbnail size and haptic feedback.

This information is not transmitted to us or anyone else and is excluded from Android cloud backups and device-to-device transfers. Thumbnails are kept in memory only.

Deleting it: use Forget project (or Settings → Manage saved projects → Forget all projects) to delete a project’s data and release the access permissions it no longer needs. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Forgetting a project never deletes your photos or saved copies.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PhotoSeq is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and share the following with Google to deliver, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud and abuse:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android device advertising identifier)Ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and, if permitted, personalised ads
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention, showing ads relevant to your region
Device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version, network type)Ad delivery, compatibility and measurement
Ad interactions (ads shown, taps) and diagnostic data from the ads SDKMeasurement, reporting, fraud prevention, SDK performance

We do not receive personal information about you from Google; we only see aggregated reports about ad performance. The ads SDK never receives your photos, file names or project data.

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent: In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other places where it is required, the app uses Google’s consent tool to ask for your choice before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices in the app.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads). Ads may still appear, but they will not be personalised.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, through the AdMob and consent SDKs, as described in section 3. When you choose to share photos or export a summary from the app, the files go only to the app or location you pick.

6. Data security and retention

Project data stays in the app’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read, until you forget the project or uninstall the app. Data sent by the Google ads SDK is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS) and is retained by Google according to its own policies.

7. Children

PhotoSeq is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at selfiecoach@gmail.com and we will help address it.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal information on our own servers, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete all app data on your device as described in section 2, and manage ad-related data through the choices in section 3. For data processed by Google, you can use the tools described in Google’s privacy policy. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have additional rights; contact us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app’s features or the services it uses change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s Google Play release notes.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your privacy, email selfiecoach@gmail.com.
